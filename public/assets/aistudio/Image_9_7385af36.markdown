## Brand & Style

### Personality & Emotional Response
The design system embodies empathy, reassurance, academic credibility, and discreet approachability. Designed for SMA Muhammadiyah 1 Palangkaraya's Guidance and Counseling (BK) unit under Ibu Dewi Kumala Sari, S.Pd., the interface balances two critical user profiles:
1. **Students (Gen Z high schoolers):** Who require a non-intimidating, safe, non-judgmental space to explore career aspirations, self-development, and psychological counseling without stigma.
2. **School Leadership, Parents, and Accreditation Assessors:** Who demand structural rigor, accountability, systematic documentation under *Kurikulum Merdeka*, and professional Islamic institutional integrity.

### Aesthetic Movement
The aesthetic synthesizes **Soft Organic Minimalism** with **Warm Academic Editorialism**. It eschews sterile institutional blues and cold grays in favor of biophilic sage tones and warm paper-like surfaces. Subtle botanical/structural grounding, human-scale typography, and low-contrast borders foster emotional safety, focus, and transparency.

## Colors

### Palette Strategy
The color system operates strictly in light mode to preserve clarity, warmth, and visual comfort.

- **Primary (`#2D4A3E` - Deep Forest Green):** Anchors institutional authority, trust, and academic grounding. Used for principal navigation bars, dominant heading titles, primary action buttons, and critical status labels.
- **Secondary (`#608066` - Sage Green):** Evokes emotional regulation, calm, balance, and growth. Applied to interactive focus states, secondary buttons, subheadings, active pill chips, and supportive illustrations.
- **Tertiary (`#C29B38` - Subtle Muhammadiyah Gold):** A respectful, controlled nod to the solar insignia of Muhammadiyah. Utilized sparingly for achievement badges, accreditation highlights, urgent announcements, and starred evaluation metrics.
- **Neutral Surface Foundation (`#F8F6F0` to `#FAF9F5` - Warm Cream / Parchment):** Prevents the clinical glare of stark white (`#FFFFFF`). White is reserved exclusively for elevated card surfaces to create gentle visual stepping without deep shadows.
- **Text & Contrast:** Primary text rests on `#1A2821` (Deep Pine Charcoal) for high legibility (AAA contrast), while muted descriptive copy uses `#4F5E56`.

## Typography

### Typographic Rationale
**Plus Jakarta Sans** is selected as the unified typeface family across display, body, and UI labels. Developed in Indonesia, its modern geometric architecture with open counters and soft terminals strikes a balanced intersection:
- Welcoming, contemporary, and friendly enough for adolescent students navigating confidential counseling appointments.
- Impeccably structured, highly legible, and crisp for formal school assessment reports, *Kurikulum Merdeka* operational plans (RPL BK), and administrative ledgers.

### Scale Rules
- Maintain generous line-heights (`1.5` to `1.6` on body copy) to maintain a calming, unhurried cadence.
- Headings use optical kerning with subtle negative letter spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to ensure title lockups feel cohesive and deliberate.
- Captions and metadata labels utilize clean uppercase or semi-bold sentence case with light positive tracking for fast scannability.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column responsive fluid grid inside a centered max-width container of `1200px`. Gutters are fixed at `1.5rem` (`24px`), with outer canvas padding set to `2.5rem` (`40px`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) margins. Sidebar schedules and counseling dossiers transition to stacked layouts.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) margin gutters.

### Rhythm & Vertical Cadence
Spacing relies strictly on an 8-point base rhythm. Section padding prioritizes breathing room (`4rem` to `6rem` vertical margins between sections) to preserve psychological spaciousness, reducing anxiety when users read sensitive consultation procedures or crisis intervention hotlines.

## Elevation & Depth

### Philosophy: Tonal Surfaces over Heavy Drop Shadows
To reflect honesty, approachability, and grounded serenity, the system avoids dramatic drop shadows and heavy gloss. Depth is constructed through **tonal surface stacking** combined with **diffused herbal-tinted ambient glows**.

### Elevation Scale
1. **Base Layer (Canvas):** Warm Cream (`#F8F6F0`). Solid, restful backdrop.
2. **Level 1 (Cards, Modules, Consultation Tiles):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) surface rested over the canvas, defined by a fine 1px structural stroke of Warm Sage Border (`#E5ECE6`). Shadow is negligible: `0 1px 3px rgba(45, 74, 62, 0.04)`.
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Hover, Focus Cards):** Subtle lift using an organic forest tint: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(45, 74, 62, 0.08)`. Border shifts smoothly to `#608066`.
4. **Level 3 (Modals, Booking Sheets, Confidential Dialogs):** `#FFFFFF` container with `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(31, 54, 45, 0.16)`. Accompanied by a muted backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(6px)`) in deep forest green at 40% opacity (`rgba(31, 54, 45, 0.40)`).

## Shapes

### Form Language
The visual corner treatment is set to **`2` (Rounded)**. This offers an organic, reassuring tactile softness without tipping into childish hyper-circular geometry.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Regular Buttons, List Items):** `0.5rem` (`8px`).
- **Containers & Content Cards (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (`16px`).
- **Hero Highlights, Modals & Visual Banners (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Pills & Status Badges:** Fully rounded (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical metadata from actionable rectangular modules.
